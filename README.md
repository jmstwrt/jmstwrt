# James M. Stewart
PhD Researcher at the University of Arkansas | Computational Mechanics (FEA)

### Brief Bio
I am a PhD researcher (civil/structural engineering) at the University of Arkansas working under Dr. Michelle Barry. My research focuses on nonlinear FEA in LS-DYNA, constitutive modeling, FEA mesh regularization, and physical validation. I genuinely love computational modeling, and I am fascinated by its historical development and ability to solve complex problems. My background is mainly solid mechanics/FEA, but I'm actively expanding my computational toolkit into ML, thermal, and fluids domains, with a specific interest in aerospace applications. 

### Research Interests
* Nonlinear computational modeling
* Constitutive modeling (plasticity, damage mechanics, etc)
* Thermal-structure and fluid-structure interaction
* Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
* Machine learning and applications to structural, thermal, or fluid domains
* Structural topology optimization
* Multiphysics simulation
