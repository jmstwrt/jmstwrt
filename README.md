# James M. Stewart
PhD Researcher | Computational Mechanics (FEA)

### Brief Bio
I am a PhD researcher (civil/structural engineering) at the University of Arkansas working under Dr. Michelle Barry. My research focuses on nonlinear FEA in LS-DYNA, constitutive modeling, FEA mesh regularization, and physical validation. I genuinely love computational modeling, and I am fascinated by its historical development and ability to solve complex problems. My background is mainly solid mechanics/FEA, but I'm actively expanding my computational toolkit into ML, thermal, and fluids domains, and I'm specifically interested in aerospace applications. 

### Research Interests
* Nonlinear computational modeling
* Constitutive modeling (plasticity, damage mechanics, etc)
* Thermal-structure and fluid-structure interaction
* Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
* Machine learning and applications to structural, thermal, or fluid domains
* Structural topology optimization
* Multiphysics simulation

## Featured Projects
* [Monte Carlo Simulation: How long does a game of "war" take?](https://github.com/jmstwrt/monte_carlo_modeling): A simple Python-based Monte Carlo script I built wondering why my card game was taking forever. Ultimately, it showed how injecting real-world entropy (unmodeled human behavior) into an overly idealized deterministic model yielded much more accurate results.
